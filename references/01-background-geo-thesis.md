## 1. The shift: from search to recommendation｜从搜索到推荐

**EN** — When a shopper's entry point stops being Amazon, Google or a social feed, and becomes a direct question to ChatGPT, Gemini or Perplexity — *"I've been sleeping badly, help me pick a good pillow"*, *"what should a first-time owner buy for a senior dog"* — the sequence changes. The AI first understands the need, then proposes an approach, then names brands. **The AI recommends; the user decides.**

**中文** — 当用户的购物入口不再只是 Amazon、Google 或社媒平台，而是直接向 AI 提问，AI 会先理解需求，再给出方案，最后推荐品牌。这个过程中，**AI 负责推荐，用户负责决策**。

The competitive question therefore moves:

| Old question｜过去 | New question｜现在 |
|---|---|
| Does the user know me?｜用户是否知道我？ | Is the AI willing to recommend me?｜AI 是否愿意推荐我？ |
| Can I be found?｜能否被搜到？ | Can I be quoted?｜能否被引用？ |
| Do I rank?｜排名如何？ | Am I part of the answer?｜是否成为答案的一部分？ |

This is why the work is called *Generative Engine Optimization* rather than search optimization. You are not competing for a position on a results page. You are competing to be **a component of a generated answer**.

<a name="2"></a>
## 2. Cultivating AI mindshare alongside consumer mindshare｜同时经营用户心智与 AI 心智

**EN** — Content marketing used to have one job: seed desire and build trust with people. It now has a second: make content legible to how AI understands and cites, so that when an engine surveys a large body of material it can reliably identify *what problem this brand solves*, *for what situation*, and *why it deserves recommendation*.

**中文** — 过去内容营销主要是给人种草、给人建立信任；现在还要多做一层工作：让内容符合 AI 的理解和引用方式，让 AI 在看到大量内容资产时，能够稳定识别品牌解决了什么问题、适合什么场景、为什么值得推荐。

**This is additive, not substitutive**（这不是替代关系，而是叠加关系）. Brands still need narrative, resonance, seeding and platform conversion. But every video, every article, every campaign beat should stop being judged purely on short-term traffic. Each should also leave a trace that is **identifiable, quotable and summarizable** to an AI, accumulating into long-term asset-grade content.

The practical consequence: a monthly calendar is no longer a list of things to publish. It is a list of things to publish **that also each deposit one piece of durable semantic evidence.**

<a name="3"></a>
## 3. From category keywords to expressed intent｜从品类词到意图表达

The larger change AI commerce brings is the move from *searching a category term* to *expressing an intent*.

| Era | User types / says | What wins |
|---|---|---|
| Search | "oil control cleanser" | Keyword coverage, rank position |
| AI | "my face gets oily by noon and everything I try makes it worse, what should I do?" | Owning the *situation*, having a structured answer to it |

Users won't only ask "best {product category}". They will ask about specific situations, problems and worries:

- Can {product type} actually replace {the incumbent approach}?
- Is {product} worth it if I only use it {frequency}?
- {Symptom description} — does {product category} actually help?
- How do I get {other household member} to genuinely take over {task}?
- What do I do when {emotionally loaded failure state} happens?

**These situations, problems and worries are the queries.**（这些场景、问题和担心，就是 GEO 工作里的 query。）

> Brands are no longer fighting for a single category shelf — they are fighting for **scenario answers**. They are no longer only managing attention — they are managing **intent entry points**. Whoever converts user intent into clear queries earliest has the best chance of being visible when the AI answers and recommends.
> 未来品牌抢占的不是单一品类货架，而是场景答案；不是只经营用户注意力，而是经营意图入口。

This is why Step 1 of the workflow is **intent research, not keyword research**, and why Step 2 organizes those queries into a structure the AI can understand:

> user question → reason → answer → recommendation logic → why this brand/product fits
> 用户问题 → 原因 → 答案 → 推荐逻辑 → 品牌/产品为什么适合

An Answer Unit is therefore not a content template. It is **the brand's structured explanation to the AI of why it deserves to be recommended.**

<a name="4"></a>
## 4. Two asset classes: narrative vs evidence｜叙事资产与证据资产

| Asset class | Audience | Job | Typical formats | GEO coupling |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Narrative**｜叙事资产 | Humans | Make people like, trust and feel seen | TVC, KV, street interviews, trend content, emotional campaign, brand values films | Mostly Light / Medium |
| **Evidence**｜证据资产 | AI + humans | Give the AI a reason to recommend, give the user a basis to decide | Expert answers, comparisons, checklists, how-to, deep reviews, forum answers, usage reports | Mostly Heavy / Medium |

> **Narrative earns trust; evidence earns the recommendation. Tell humans stories, feed AI evidence.**
> **叙事解决信任，证据解决推荐。对人要讲故事，对 AI 要喂证据。**

The single most common mistake in early GEO programs is treating this as a hierarchy — trying to convert all narrative content into evidence content. It isn't a hierarchy; it's a portfolio. A brand with only evidence content has no desire and no distinctiveness. A brand with only narrative content is invisible to the recommendation layer. The workflow's Heavy / Medium / Light coupling exists precisely so both can be produced and **judged by their own standards**.

<a name="5"></a>
## 5. Why this is a semantic verification loop, not a content task｜这是语义验证闭环

The workflow is defensible because it closes a loop:

> real user language → AI query → extractable Answer Unit → platform citation surface → GEO + platform KPI recovery
> 真实用户语言 → AI query → 可抽取 AU → 平台 citation surface → GEO/platform KPI 回收

It resolves the failure modes of first-generation GEO work:

- **No more giant static query inventories** built for their own sake — queries must earn their place with listening evidence.
- **No more AUs detached from real scenarios** — every AU traces to a source thread.
- **No more platform plans that are just content calendars** — every post carries an angle, a funnel role and a citation surface.
- **No more mixing GEO KPIs with platform KPIs** — two families, tracked separately, bridged on Query ID.
- **No more every-platform-does-everything** — roles are divided.

The keystone is the **Semantic North Star**. Because one sentence governs every AU, every platform and every product line, all of it converges on a single AI cognitive outcome instead of dispersing into unrelated claims.

<a name="6"></a>
## 6. Collaboration boundary: what a GEO workstream is and isn't｜项目边界

A Social GEO workstream optimizes content topic selection, message seeding and platform layout so that social content contributes to GEO. **It is not traditional social media marketing and it does not take over day-to-day platform operations.**

More precisely: it manages **how the message at each marketing-calendar beat gets translated into a GEO query and seeded into the social content calendar.** Every campaign, product launch and seasonal beat should stop being a one-off impression and start sedimenting into corpus that AI search can identify and cite.

| Cadence | Input | Output | Function |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Monthly** | Marketing calendar, campaign beats, product launch needs, last month's dashboard | GEO-optimized social calendar with content angles | Translate each calendar beat into a trackable Query ID / AU / platform angle |
| **Weekly** | That week's content, scripts, captions, covers, briefs, platform assets | Optimized content + GEO suggestions | Keep query, AU and citation surface unbroken at the execution level |

Existing platform execution flows do not change. The GEO workstream supplies topic selection, angle, query seeding and format optimization from a GEO perspective. That framing matters commercially: it positions the work as a **strategic layer above execution**, not a replacement for the in-house social team.

<a name="7"></a>
## 7. The three long-term GEO assets｜三大长期资产

These are Owner-Layer assets — they compound, they are not re-bought each month, and they are what makes the program worth retaining rather than project-billing.

| Asset | Definition | Role in this workflow | Update cadence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Intent → Query database**｜意图-查询数据库 | A living map from emotion words, scenario words and verbatim user language to AI-style queries | The core asset of Step 1 — turns listening into a coverable problem space | Monthly additions, quarterly overhaul |
| **Answer Unit library**｜AU 模板库 | A unified-structure answer for each core question: definition, reason, solution, recommendation logic, proof | The production asset of Step 2 — keeps the same query answered consistently across platforms | Biweekly additions, monthly refinement |
| **Authority knowledge base**｜权威知识库 | Structured scenario × expert principle × decision framework × product logic | Supplies credible input for YMYL scenarios so AUs don't become brand self-assertion | Quarterly expert review, monthly draw-down |

Their relationship:

> The Intent → Query database discovers **what users are asking**. The Authority knowledge base supplies **defensible grounds for the answer**. The Answer Unit library assembles the answer into **modules an AI can extract**.

## 8. Executive talk track: bridging from SEO｜向高层讲 GEO 的过桥话术

Most executives have an SEO mental model and no GEO concept. Never open with GEO cold — bridge from what they already believe.

**The bridge:**

> SEO is for people; GEO is for AI. Both run on the same three things — the right keywords, an authoritative structure, and a large body of quality corpus. The difference is what consumes the output. SEO earns a position on a results page that a human then clicks. GEO earns a place inside the answer that the AI writes, which the human then acts on. We already invest in the first. The second is the same investment, structured differently.
> SEO 给人看，GEO 给 AI 看；都基于关键词 + 权威结构 + 高质量语料。区别只在于谁来消费输出。

**The three questions the dashboard answers** — state these as the entire reporting frame, because executives reject multi-metric sprawl:

| Layer | The question | Plain-language version |
|---|---|---|
| 1 · Presence | Does the AI mention us when recommending this category? | "Do we show up at all?" |
| 2 · Reason | Is the reason it gives our north star attribute? | "Do we show up *for the right reason*?" |
| 3 · Support | Do the cited sources actually substantiate that reason? | "Is there real evidence behind it, or is it a fluke?" |

**Set the lag expectation up front.** AI citation moves on a weeks-to-months lag. Month one should be reported on leading indicators — corpus density, citation surfaces shipped, early mentions. Real Layer 1 and 2 movement is a quarterly read. Saying this *before* month one prevents the program being judged a failure on its first report.

**Frame the payoff as timing, not novelty.** Once AI commerce is mainstream, the brands that benefit first will not be the ones chasing trends. They will be the ones that laid down queries, Answer Units, product evidence, expert content and platform citation surfaces early. The purpose of the monthly loop is to convert each month's marketing calendar from one-off communication into assets that AI can identify, cite and recommend from — for years.
