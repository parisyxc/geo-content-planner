# Social GEO Content Planner｜社媒 GEO 内容规划器

**EN** — A repeatable monthly operating system that converts any brand's social media output from one-off campaign traffic into long-lived, AI-citable evidence assets.

**中文** — 一套可复用的月度操作系统，把任何品牌的社媒产出从一次性 campaign 流量，转化为可被 AI 长期识别、引用、推荐的证据型资产。

---

## Why this exists｜为什么需要这套流程

**EN** — Shopping entry points are moving from Amazon/Google/social feeds to direct questions asked of AI: *"I've been sleeping badly, help me pick a pillow."* The AI understands the need, proposes a solution, then names a brand. In that flow **the AI recommends and the user decides**. So the competitive question is no longer only "does the user know me" — it is **"is the AI willing to recommend me"**. Brands must now cultivate *AI mindshare* alongside *consumer mindshare*.

**中文** — 购物入口正在从 Amazon / Google / 社媒 feed，转移到直接向 AI 提问：「我最近睡眠不太好，帮我挑一个好枕头」。AI 先理解需求，再给方案，最后推荐品牌。这个链路里 **AI 负责推荐，用户负责决策**。品牌竞争的关键因此不只是「用户是否知道我」，而是 **「AI 是否愿意推荐我」**。品牌要在经营用户心智之外，同时经营 AI 心智。

This is **additive, not substitutive**（叠加关系，不是替代关系）. Narrative still matters. But every video, article and campaign beat should also leave a trace an AI can recognize, extract and summarize.

Two asset classes now coexist｜两类内容资产并存：

| Asset class｜资产类型 | Audience｜对象 | Job｜作用 |
|---|---|---|
| **Narrative assets**｜叙事资产 | Humans｜人 | Make people like, trust, feel seen — TVC, KV, street interviews, trend content, emotional campaign |
| **Evidence assets**｜证据资产 | AI + humans｜AI + 人 | Give the AI a *reason* to recommend, and give the user a *basis* to decide — expert answers, comparisons, checklists, how-to, reviews, forum answers |

> **Narrative earns trust; evidence earns the recommendation. Tell humans stories, feed AI evidence.**
> **叙事解决信任，证据解决推荐。对人要讲故事，对 AI 要喂证据。**

The deeper shift is from **category keywords → expressed intent**（从「搜品类词」到「表达意图」）. Users no longer type "oil-control cleanser"; they ask "my skin gets sensitive when seasons change, what won't irritate it?" Those situations, worries and comparisons **are** the queries this skill hunts for.

For the full strategic argument, worked AI-commerce examples, and how to brief a skeptical executive on this, read `references/01-background-geo-thesis.md`.

## The monthly loop｜月度四步闭环

> **Query Finding → Answer Unit Production → Platform Distribution → Monthly Track**
> 从真实用户问题出发 → 生产 AI 可抽取的答案模块 → 分发到合适平台 → 用 GEO 与平台双线 KPI 验证。

| Step | Core question｜核心问题 | Monthly artifact｜月度产出 |
|---|---|---|
| **0 · Intake** | 品牌想让 AI 记住的那**一句话**是什么？ | Semantic North Star + platform role sheet |
| **1 · Query Finding** | 真实用户在问什么？AI 未来可能回答什么？ | Query Map (Core × Sub) |
| **2 · Answer Unit** | 怎么把 query 做成可被 AI 抽取的答案模块？ | Answer Unit library |
| **3 · Distribution** | 每个 AU 变成什么平台内容，放到哪里？ | Per-platform monthly plan |
| **4 · Monthly Track** | AI 是否采用了我们的推荐理由？平台漏斗是否推进？ | GEO dashboard + funnel review → double/drop |

Suggested cadence｜建议节奏: Day 1-3 listening & Query Map · Day 4-6 Answer Units · Day 7-8 platform plans · Week 2-4 distribution, reinforcement, hit-content capture · Month end dual-KPI review and double/drop decisions.

## Step 0 · Intake and the Semantic North Star｜语义北极星

**This is the single most important step, and the most commonly skipped.** Everything downstream — which queries are worth winning, which evidence angle each AU carries, which platform angle to choose — resolves against one sentence.

### Define the Semantic North Star

Write one sentence in this shape:

> **Get AI search engines to progressively understand: {BRAND} is the {ATTRIBUTE} choice in {CATEGORY}.**
> **让 AI 搜索引擎逐步理解：{品牌} 是 {品类} 里 {某个属性} 的选择。**

The attribute must be **one** claim, defensible with real evidence, and differentiated from the obvious leader. Examples across categories:

| Category | Weak north star｜弱 | Strong north star｜强 |
|---|---|---|
| Mid-priced sleep products | "the best pillow brand" | *the smart-value choice for people who wake with neck pain but won't spend $300* |
| Sensitive-skin skincare | "a great skincare brand" | *the lowest-irritation-risk choice for reactive skin during seasonal transition* |
| Pet nutrition | "high quality pet food" | *the choice for owners managing a specific health condition without a vet-exclusive price* |

### Decompose the attribute into 5–8 proof angles

A north star said once is a slogan; said as eight different pieces of evidence it becomes a semantic pattern the AI can generalize. Decompose before writing any AU. Worked example for a **"value for money"** north star — note that none of these say "cheap":

- **smart value** — it's worth what you paid
- **practical value** — it actually gets used day to day
- **multi-function value** — one product retires several tasks
- **accessible quality** — quality and features are good, price is reachable
- **routine-saving value** — reduces repeated daily labor and household friction
- **gift value** — as a gift it isn't decorative, it's used every day

Every Answer Unit later carries **at least one** of these angles. The rule is *not* "repeat the north star in every AU" — it is **"every AU contributes one specific piece of evidence for it."**

### Confirm platform roles

Take input #3 and turn it into a role sheet: for each platform, its **role in the matrix**, its **current business objective**, and its **weakest funnel layer**. If the brand hasn't diagnosed funnel weakness, use the platform-role defaults in `references/04-platform-roles-library.md` and mark the diagnosis as provisional. Later, in Step 3, the weakest layer drives each platform's angle choice.

### Gate before proceeding

Do not start listening until you can state, in one line each: the north star, its proof angles, this month's priority category or product line, and the campaign beats that must ship regardless (TVC, KV, launch — these are non-negotiable calendar items; GEO's job is to rewrite their *message*, not to cancel them).

## Step 1 · Query Finding｜查询发现

**This is intent research, not keyword research.**（这不是关键词研究，是意图研究。）The goal is not search-volume coverage — it is **occupying the decision nodes on the user's path**（占据用户决策路径中的关键问题节点）.

The question to answer:

> When AI answers which real user questions does it have a natural opening to recommend this brand — *and* to understand the reason as our north star attribute?

Reddit is the primary listening source because it uniquely contains scenario-rich real questions, emotionally dense user phrasing, natural-language question forms, and a high volume of comparison / complaint / help-seeking / experience-sharing threads that map cleanly onto AI prompts. Substitute or supplement with the equivalent forum in your market where Reddit is thin (Xiaohongshu, Quora, Discord servers, category-specific forums, app-store reviews, customer-service transcripts).

### Three listening layers｜三层 listening 结构

| Layer | Priority | Scope｜范围 | Rule｜执行规则 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **L1 · Priority category** | Highest | 本月最想打的品类/品线 | Mandatory. This is the month's main battlefield. 必做。 |
| **L2 · Campaign & product beats** | Secondary | 本月 campaign 节点、新品、节日、相关使用场景 | Do it, but in service of the campaign and this month's product mix. 服务 campaign，不脱轨。 |
| **L3 · Hot / long-tail scenarios** | Optional | 当月热点、生活方式、出行、季节场景 | Only when listening surfaces genuine resonance. **Never pad the list to hit a number.** 不为凑数硬做。 |

### Two execution tracks｜1A / 1B 双轨

Different product lines enter at different points. Pick the track from what the client actually handed you:

**Track 1A · Query Discovery** — *client gives products, we find queries.* For a line entering GEO for the first time, a new category, or when there's no query direction yet.
`Client provides line/product/competitors → full listening sweep → friction synthesis → Core Query clustering → Sub-Query expansion → client summary → client confirms → Step 2.`
Heaviest workload; this is what builds a line's query pool from zero.

**Track 1B · Query Validation** — *client gives queries, we find the insight.* For lines that already have an evergreen query list (from SEO, customer service FAQ logs, an existing content matrix) that needs real-user evidence and enrichment.
`Client provides query list → targeted listening against those queries only → backfill source post, real user language, friction dimensions, competitor mentions, proof angle → light clustering (dedupe + dimension completion) → Step 2.`
Roughly 30–40% of 1A's workload. The value is *enriching* with real evidence, not rediscovering.

Rule of thumb: input is "product name + competitors" → 1A. Input is "query list + questions we want to cover" → 1B. A line typically runs 1A in month one and 1B for increments thereafter.

### Query Map fields

Record every query with the fields in `assets/query-map-template.csv`. The point is **not** to log the query — it is to preserve the Reddit evidence, engagement intensity and comment insight alongside it, so that priority calls are made on evidence rather than taste. Full field definitions and the friction taxonomy live in `references/02-reddit-listening-playbook.md`.

Four signals that should drive priority beyond raw relevance:

- **High karma / upvotes** → the problem has group resonance, not just individual complaint
- **High comment count** → it provokes discussion; good for multi-angle AUs
- **Comments converge** → stable recommendation logic is possible
- **Comments diverge sharply** → ideal for comparison, decision framework or myth-busting content

### The closing move: Core Query Clustering｜核心 query 聚类

**Never hand off a flat query list.** Listening returns fragments of real user language; what GEO actually needs to occupy are the *entry-level decision questions* in AI answers. So after building the raw pool, always run one clustering pass to produce:

> **M Core Queries × N Sub-Queries / evaluation dimensions each**

| Layer | Definition | How it's used |
|---|---|---|
| **Core Query** | The entry-level decision question a user genuinely asks an AI | Anchors one standalone AU. This is what the client confirms first. |
| **Sub-Query / Evaluation Dimension** | A dimension the Core AU must cover — price, alternatives, frequency of use, risk, who it suits, trust evidence | Not a standalone AU title. Becomes paragraphs, FAQ entries, carousel slides, checklist rows, reply angles. |
| **Dimension Note** | Supplementary detail, edge condition, comment insight, caveat | Crafter reference only — supplies examples, proof, warnings, platform angles. |
| **Merged / Deleted** | Duplicates, non-user queries, tactical goals disguised as queries | Keep the audit trail; exclude from production. |

The evolution is `Raw Listening → Flat Query Pool` upgraded to `Raw Listening → Friction Cluster → Core Query Architecture → AU Handoff`.

Why this matters: without it, the client (or the AU crafter) assumes every logged query needs its own AU, which conflates *decisions* with *evaluation dimensions* and multiplies production cost several-fold for no GEO gain.

Execution guidance: synthesize friction per batch before clustering · typically 1 Core Query per batch, 2 only if genuinely warranted · keep 3–5 of the most critical Sub-Queries per Core · demote the rest to Dimension Notes · hand off only Core + Sub to Step 2 · in client review, confirm Core Query *direction* first, Sub-Query *coverage* second.

> The goal of Query Finding is not to collect the most queries. It is to distill, from real user language, the Core Decision Queries that AI can answer, the brand can occupy, and content can be produced against. **Raw query is evidence; Core Query is the entry point; Sub-Query is the AU's expansion axis.**

### Query ID — the spine of the whole chain｜ID 是全链路的脊柱

Format: `{PERIOD}-{LINE}-{THEME}-{NN}` — e.g. `JUL-SLEEP-VFM-01`, `AUG-SKIN-SENSITIVE-03`.

One Query ID → one or more AU IDs (`{QueryID}-AU01`) → fanned out across multiple platforms and formats. Every post in Step 3 and every citation or funnel datapoint in Step 4 hangs back onto that ID. Without this spine you cannot answer the only question that matters at month end: *which proof angle, under which query, via which platform's content, became an AI citation source?*

**Multi-line dedupe.** When several product lines run Step 1 in parallel they share one index, distinguished by the line segment of the ID. Before entering Step 2, run one cross-line check: identical Core Query found by two lines → produce **one** AU covering both lines' products. Similar Core Queries at different angles → keep as separate AUs that cross-reference each other.

## Step 2 · Answer Unit Production｜答案模块生产

An Answer Unit turns a real query into a structurally clear, semantically stable **standard answer module** that an AI can extract wholesale.

Two non-negotiable conditions: the query came from **real listening**, not imagination; and the AU contributes, from some angle, to the north star. Again — not every AU repeats the north star, **every AU contributes one concrete piece of evidence for it.**

### AU structure

> Question → User Scenario → Reason → Answer → Recommendation Logic → North Star Evidence → Product-Specific Proof

| Field | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Query ID / AU ID | Inherited spine, for distribution and attribution |
| Query | The question the AI may be asked |
| Why users ask this | Real context from listening |
| Short Answer | One passage an AI can lift directly |
| Decision Criteria | What users actually weigh here |
| Brand Fit | Why this brand suits this question |
| **North Star Evidence** | Which specific proof angle this query demonstrates |
| **Product-Specific Proof** | How this month's featured product demonstrates that angle |
| Citation-Ready Paragraph | A passage that stands alone without surrounding context |
| Platform Use | Which platforms and formats this belongs on |

Execution requirements: North Star Evidence can never stop at the brand-level slogan — it must name *which* proof angle this query demonstrates. If the AU serves a campaign or a product launch, Product-Specific Proof is required, and it is not a spec dump — it explains *why this particular product supports this query's answer*. If no featured product applies this month, write `TBD · no campaign product assigned` and resolve it before platform allocation.

### Five GEO content templates

| Template | Fits | Example shape |
|---|---|---|
| Q&A Answer | Direct AI questions | "Is a {product} worth it for {situation}?" |
| Comparison | Decision moments | "{A} vs {B}: which actually saves more {resource}?" |
| Checklist | Save & search behavior | "{Situation} checklist for {audience}" |
| Routine / How-to | Workflow problems | "How to handle {recurring task} in {constraint}" |
| Scenario Proof | Emotion, campaign, gifting | "Why a practical {occasion} gift reduces {load}" |

### Authority layer for YMYL categories｜权威层

Health, childcare, finance, safety and other YMYL categories carry extra risk and extra opportunity: AI engines lean conservative and prefer authoritative sources. The move is not "add an endorsement badge" — it is **structured authority expression**: source binding ("Certified {expert type} generally advise that…"), principle statements ("Under {standard}, selection should prioritize…"), and decision frameworks ("frequent use → portable device; building capacity → dual electric"). Expert > influencer > anonymous user in citation weight.

Correspondingly, **never absolutize in a YMYL category**. Where listening shows a genuine split ("this depends heavily on the individual"), the honest, conditional framing is *also* the more citable one, because it survives AI's conservatism filter. Full craft guidance, anti-patterns and worked before/after rewrites: `references/03-answer-unit-craft.md`.

## Step 3 · Platform Distribution｜平台分发

AUs cannot stay in an internal doc. They must become platform-native content that users will watch, save and discuss — and that AI engines may later quote or summarize.

### Match GEO coupling to content type — don't force every post into a full AU

`Pillar` is the platform's operating structure — it determines what a platform is *supposed* to publish. `GEO` is a semantic enrichment mechanism — it determines which of those posts deserve to be reshaped into extractable assets. **These are not one-to-one.** Forcing a full AU onto every pillar produces stilted content and wastes production capacity.

| Coupling | Definition | Typical content | How to execute |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Heavy** | Full GEO asset. Must carry Query ID / AU ID / proof angle / citation-ready phrasing | Expert content, product-explanation posts, checklists, infographics, comparison & decision pins, how-to, deep reviews, forum research answers | Rewrite to the full AU structure |
| **Medium** | Semantic seeding. No full AU, but needs a query hook, scenario language, a proof angle or comment prompt | Real-resonance posts, tips & usage sharing, testimonials, UGC, conversation prompts | Split the AU into hook + scenario + user language + light answer or CTA; drive save / comment / search |
| **Light** | GEO hygiene only. Serves platform traffic, brand reach, or mandatory campaign shipping | Trend content, street interviews, brand TVC / KV / values campaigns, pure entertainment collabs | Optimize title, first caption line, subtitle keywords, cover text, pinned comment only |

This classification is what protects narrative content from being wrongly judged in Step 4 — a TVC cut was never supposed to generate an AI citation.

### Let the weakest funnel layer choose each platform's angle

Platform role decides *what content it carries*; **this month's angle and format should be driven by that platform's weakest funnel layer.** Don't ship one generic north-star post to all seven platforms — use the proof angles to patch whichever layer is currently blocked on each.

The procedure when filling in `Post-level Angle`: (1) identify which layer this post must patch, (2) pick one angle from the proof-angle library, (3) pair it with that platform's native format. Each post then serves two goals at once — patching platform funnel weakness (KPI 2) and laying down one citation surface (KPI 1).

The platform role library, funnel-weakness diagnostic table, AU-entry-point mapping (question hook / definition / why-it-matters / recommendation logic / product fit → which platforms) and per-platform format references live in `references/04-platform-roles-library.md`. Read it before writing any platform plan.

### Forum platforms are decision platforms first, citation sources second

Where the brand uses Reddit or an equivalent community, its **first** role is not "a place to post brand content" and not even "a citation source." It is the platform where people actually *research → find answers → pick options → ask for opinion.* Users arrive already carrying a real problem, a comparison set, budget concerns and risk worries.

So the content must first help them judge: does this suit me, when is it worth it, when is it *not*, what else should I consider. Only once it is a useful decision answer does it acquire its second layer of value — citation surface, listening seed, and proof field in a peer-to-peer voice.

Execution order: `real high-intent thread → decision-grade answer → suits/doesn't-suit caveats → brand fit appears naturally → sediments into citation surface.`

Compliance floor before any community publishing: no fake personas · disclose any paid / brand / agency / affiliate / gift relationship · preserve caveats in regulated categories and avoid anything reading as professional advice · respect each sub's self-promotion rules (many require no-brand, no-link) · after publishing, backfill the final link plus 7-day comments / upvotes / sentiment / removal status.

### Per-platform monthly plan

One page per platform. State role and AUs carried, then this month's quantity, then a post-level table. Fields: `Platform Role · Selected Query Touchpoints · Monthly Quantity · Post-level Angle · Simple Format · Citation Surface · Funnel Role (L1–L4) · KPI`.

Post-level rows use `assets/platform-monthly-plan-template.md` — `No. | Query ID | AU ID | Query/AU | Post-level Angle | Simple Format | Funnel Role | Notes`. This is deliberately light: the execution team needs the angle and the format, not finished copy.

**Angle rule** — every angle must prove the north star from a *different* direction, never restate one line. Rotate across the proof angles defined in Step 0.

**Volume framework** — set monthly quantity from the platform's existing publishing cadence and production capacity, not from a template. Sanity check: heavy-GEO platforms (search-driven, evergreen, long-shelf-life) deserve *more* volume than they usually get, because their assets compound; feed-driven platforms deserve consistency over spikes.

## Step 4 · Monthly Track & Optimization｜月度追踪与优化

Two KPI families, tracked separately, then bridged.

### KPI 1 · GEO layers

| Layer | The question | Example metric |
|---|---|---|
| **Brand Presence** | Does the AI mention us at all in category recommendations? | Category prompt hit rate |
| **Semantic Reason** | Is the *reason* it recommends us our north star attribute? | North-star semantic match rate |
| **Citation Support** | Do the cited sources actually substantiate that reason? | Citation source count · quality · content fit |

### KPI 2 · Platform funnel layers

`L1 Awareness` reach/views/impressions · `L2 Engagement` save/share/comments/completion · `L3 Consideration` profile visits, link clicks, product page visits · `L4 Conversion` traffic, conversion rate, assisted conversion, ROI.

L3 is usually the most diagnostic layer: *"I'll come back to this"* signals (save, subscribe, follow) predict purchase intent better than *"I agree with this"* signals (like, share).

### The bridge: Citation Source × Funnel

Read separately, the two KPIs produce either "great platform numbers but nothing citable" or "GEO is rising and we can't say which platform earned it." Cross them on Query ID, and at month end answer both questions per platform at once.

**Attribution must respect coupling level.** Heavy and Medium content enters both KPI 1 and KPI 2. **Light content enters KPI 2 only** and carries no citation target — otherwise TVC, KV, trend and mandatory campaign posts get scored as failures for not producing something they were never designed to produce.

**Leading vs lagging.** AI citation lags weeks to months. Read *leading* indicators monthly (proof-angle corpus density, citation surfaces shipped, early mentions) and read real Layer 1/2 movement on a *quarterly* trend. Seeing no semantic shift in month one is expected, not a failure.

### Optimization moves

AI mentions us but for the wrong reason → strengthen that query's evidence. AI recommends a competitor → analyze which sources it cites and why, then match the scenario or beat the evidence. No brand is stably recommended for a query → keep reinforcing; it's an open field. A query shows weak resonance and low citation potential → demote or drop next month. An AU type is consistently adopted by AI or platforms → double down cross-platform. High-comment questions from the platforms → feed straight back into next month's Query Finding.

Dashboard structure, metric definitions and the double/drop decision rules: `references/05-tracking-and-kpi.md`.

## Risks and corrections｜主要风险与修正

| Risk | Correction |
|---|---|
| North star collapses into one flat adjective (e.g. "cheap") | Decompose into 5–8 differentiated proof angles at Step 0 and rotate them |
| Listening returns scattered fragments | Every query retains source URL, post title, author, sub, upvotes, comment count, verbatim language, comment summary, friction insight |
| AUs read like brand copy | Answer the question first; recommendation logic appears only after the answer stands on its own |
| Platform plans balloon in complexity | One page per platform: role and quantity first, then a post-level angle + format table |
| GEO KPIs move slowly and morale drops | Track citation *source* and *content* enrichment as leading indicators; read semantic shift quarterly |
| Platform performance and GEO drift apart | Review funnel and GEO dashboard together each month end, cross-referenced on Query ID |
| Every pillar forced into a full AU | Apply Heavy / Medium / Light coupling; let Light content be Light |

## First-run guidance｜第一次跑这个流程

Keep month one light. Resist the urge to build a giant static query inventory — that was the failure mode of the previous generation of GEO work.

1. Lock the Semantic North Star and its proof angles with the client **before** any listening.
2. Run L1 only, on the single highest-priority product line.
3. Cluster down to **8–15 Core Queries** total. Fewer, stronger.
4. Write AUs only for Core Queries, each with a real listening source and one named proof angle.
5. Distribute the strongest AUs across platforms with post-level angle + format — no finished copy yet.
