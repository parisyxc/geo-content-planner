## 1. How to use this library｜使用方式

Input #3 of the skill is *platform positioning — which platforms and their role.* Where the brand has already defined roles, use theirs. Where they haven't, propose from the defaults below and **have them confirm before you build plans** — a platform role assigned by the agency and never ratified by the brand becomes the first thing disputed at review.

Two things this library is not: it is not a ranking of platforms, and it is not a fixed set. Add, remove and substitute for the brand's market. What must survive substitution is the **structure**: every platform gets one role, one business objective, one weakest funnel layer, and one coupling profile.

**Role assignment discipline:** no two platforms should share a role. If two do, one of them has no reason to exist in the matrix and its budget is better spent doubling the other. Forcing distinct roles is what stops the plan degenerating into "post everything everywhere."

## 3. GEO coupling: Heavy / Medium / Light｜GEO 耦合层级

`Pillar` is the platform's operating structure — it determines what a platform is *supposed* to publish (campaign beats, brand films, trend participation, community management). `GEO` is a semantic enrichment mechanism — it determines which of those posts deserve to be reshaped into extractable assets.

**These are not one-to-one, and forcing them to be produces stilted content and wasted capacity.**

| Coupling | Definition | Typical content | Execution |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Heavy** | Full GEO asset. Must carry Query ID / AU ID / proof angle / citation-ready phrasing | Expert content, product-explanation posts, checklists, infographics, comparison and decision pins, how-to, deep reviews, forum research answers, official answer capsules | Rewrite to the full AU structure: Question → Reason → Answer → Recommendation Logic → Proof → Product-Specific Proof |
| **Medium** | Semantic seeding. No full AU, but needs a query hook, scenario language, a proof angle, or a comment prompt | Real-resonance posts, tips and usage sharing, testimonials, UGC, conversation prompts, scenario shorts | Split the AU into hook + scenario + user language + light answer or CTA; drive save / comment / search behavior |
| **Light** | GEO hygiene only. Serves platform traffic, brand reach or mandatory campaign shipping | Trend participation, street interviews, brand TVC / KV, values campaigns, pure entertainment collaborations | Optimize title, first caption line, subtitle keywords, cover text and pinned comment only. Do not force an answer module. |

**Campaign beats are non-negotiable calendar items.** Brand films, key visuals, launches and values campaigns must ship regardless of GEO. GEO's job is to rewrite their *message* into something that can sediment a query or a citation surface — not to cancel or delay them. This framing is also what keeps the in-house social team collaborative rather than defensive.

The classification's most important function is downstream: it protects narrative content from being wrongly scored in Step 4. A trend cut was never designed to produce an AI citation and must not be judged as if it were.

## 4. Funnel-weakness driven angle selection｜漏斗弱点驱动

Platform role decides *what content a platform carries*. **This month's angle and format should be driven by that platform's weakest funnel layer.**

The failure mode this prevents: shipping one generic north-star post to every platform. Instead, use the proof angles to patch whichever layer is currently blocked on each platform.

| Weakest layer | What it means | What to ship |
|---|---|---|
| **L1 Awareness** | Not reaching enough of the right people | Conflict hooks, trend-native formats, keyword-forward covers, search-optimized titles |
| **L2 Engagement** | Reaching but not moving anyone | Higher-resonance scenarios, comment-provoking questions, more specific situations, stronger first three seconds |
| **L3 Consideration** | Engaging but no intent signal | **Savable** decision content — comparisons, checklists, decision frameworks, expert explanation. This is the layer proof angles serve best. |
| **L4 Conversion** | Intent but no action | Clear link paths, testimonial and proof content, A/B tested value framings, offer clarity |

**L3 is usually the most diagnostic layer.** "I'll come back to this" signals — save, subscribe, follow — predict purchase intent far better than "I agree with this" signals like like and share. A platform with strong L2 and flat L3 has a resonance-without-utility problem, and the fix is almost always decision-grade savable content, which is exactly what Heavy-coupled AUs produce.

**Worked diagnostic table** — fill one of these per platform each month:

| Platform | Weakest layer | This month's question | Angle × format | Core KPI |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| *e.g. feed platform* | L2–L3 · engagement fine, intent invisible | Build product understanding and trust before the click | Proof angle → savable decision carousel | Save rate · profile visit |
| *e.g. discovery platform* | L1–L2 · views high, comments weak | Break out + raise hit-content probability | Proof angle hidden inside a conflict hook | Hit-content count · organic views |
| *e.g. search platform* | L1–L2 · surfaced but not opened | Strengthen cover keywords, hit the pain point, reduce brand feel | Proof angle → pain-point-cover checklist / comparison | Click rate |
| *e.g. long-form platform* | L1 & L4 · low organic, search share too low | Feed SEO/GEO, raise search-sourced traffic | Proof angle → deep comparison / how-to with keyword title | Impression CTR · search share · subscribe |
| *e.g. community platform* | L2 & L4 · exposure limited to too few communities | Expand decision-scenario coverage, then sediment citation surface | Proof angle → worth-it judgment, option comparison, peer answer | Useful discussions · high-intent replies · indexed answers |

**Usage:** when filling `Post-level Angle`, first check this table to lock *which layer this post patches*, then pick one angle from the proof-angle library, then pair it with the platform's native format. Each post then serves two goals simultaneously — patching platform funnel weakness (KPI 2) and laying one citation surface (KPI 1).

## 5. AU entry-point → platform mapping｜AU 入口对照

An AU has several possible entry points. Which one you lead with determines which platform it fits. This is a reference, not a rule — adjust to the month's queries, platform state and available assets.

| AU entry point | Best platforms | Why this cut |
|---|---|---|
| **Question hook** | Forums, short-text, discovery video, feed video | Opening with the user's real phrasing makes the content read as a response, not an ad |
| **Definition** | Long-form video, search platforms, official FAQ, official community posts | Defining the selection criteria tells both AI and user what this content answers |
| **Why it matters** | Feed carousels, community platforms, short-text, forum comments | Explains the pain and the household context; strongest for shared-responsibility and workload topics |
| **Recommendation logic** | Long-form video, search platforms, official community posts, professional networks | Sediments a reusable decision framework instead of a single selling point |
| **Product fit** | Feed, long-form video, search platforms, official community | Connects to product and audience *after* the question has been answered |

The ordering matters: leading with product fit on a question-hook platform is the single most common reason GEO content underperforms natively. Lead with the entry point the platform rewards; the product arrives later in the same piece.

## 7. Community platforms: decision venue first｜社群平台先是决策平台

Where the brand participates in Reddit or an equivalent community, its **first** role is not "a place to post brand content," and not even "a citation source." It is where people actually:

> research → find answers → pick options → ask for opinion

Users arrive already carrying a real problem, a comparison set, budget concerns, use scenarios and risk worries. Content must first help them judge: does this suit me, when is it worth it, when is it *not* worth it, what alternatives exist.

Only once it is a genuinely useful decision answer does it acquire its second layer of value:

- **Citation surface** — real discussion and structured answers an AI may later quote or summarize
- **Listening seed** — continued discovery of new queries, friction language, comparison angles and caveats
- **Proof field** — evidence for the north star in a peer-to-peer voice, which carries differently than brand voice

Execution order:

> real high-intent thread → decision-grade answer → suits / doesn't-suit caveats → brand fit appears naturally → sediments into citation surface

**Two-track structure.** Where the brand runs both an official presence and a broader participation program: the official track carries authority — expert answers, AMAs, official answer capsules for queries that need institutional weight. The participation track works inside existing high-intent threads, offering peer-level decision frameworks around discovered queries. **The participation track is not covert advertising** — its output is judgment frameworks, and its measure is whether the reply is genuinely useful to the person who asked.

**Compliance floor, non-negotiable before any community publishing:**

- No fabricated personas
- Disclose any paid / brand / agency / affiliate / gift relationship
- In regulated categories, preserve caveats and avoid anything reading as professional advice
- Respect each community's self-promotion rules — many require no-brand, no-link participation, and violating this gets the account banned and the brand named
- New accounts need a warm-up period of genuine participation before any brand-adjacent contribution
- Keep brand-mentioning content a small minority of total contribution
- After publishing, backfill the final link plus 7-day comments, upvotes, sentiment and removal status

## 8. Volume framing｜数量框架

Set monthly quantity from the platform's existing publishing cadence and the team's production capacity — not from a template. Then sanity-check against two principles:

**Compounding platforms deserve more than they usually get.** Search-driven, evergreen, long-shelf-life platforms produce assets whose value accumulates; feed-driven platforms produce assets whose value decays in days. Most brands' allocation is the inverse of this, because feed platforms are where the visible engagement is. If the north star is a long-term AI-recommendation outcome, correcting this allocation is one of the highest-leverage moves available.

**Consistency beats spikes on feed platforms.** A steady cadence that the team can actually sustain outperforms a heavy month followed by a gap, both algorithmically and for the corpus density that GEO depends on.

Record the reasoning behind each platform's number in the monthly plan. Quantities set without reasoning get renegotiated every month.

<a name="9"></a>
## 9. Filling the monthly plan｜填写月度计划

One page per platform. State role and AUs carried, then quantity, then the post-level table.

**Page-level fields:**

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Platform Role | What this platform carries this month |
| Selected Query Touchpoints | Which queries / AUs it carries |
| Monthly Quantity | Planned volume + the reasoning |
| Weakest Funnel Layer | From the diagnostic table |
| Core KPI | The one metric this platform is judged on |

**Post-level table** (`assets/platform-monthly-plan-template.md`):

`No. | Query ID | AU ID | Query/AU | Post-level Angle | Simple Format | Funnel Role | Notes`

Deliberately light — the execution team needs the angle and the format, not finished copy. Writing full copy at this stage both slows the plan and pre-empts the platform team's craft, which is the fastest way to lose their cooperation.

**Angle rule:** every angle must prove the north star from a *different* direction. Rotate across the proof angles from Step 0. Three consecutive posts making the same argument in different formats is one post with wasted budget.
